How it works

Three programs, without jargon

It isn’t a website for looking things up. They’re “engines”: programs that run on your computer and respond when another program sends a request.

🧮 Regular tax regime calculator

É a official multiplication table of the new tax. You describe the sale (product, amount, city, date), and it returns the CBS, IBS, and Selective Tax for each item, with the calculation written out in Portuguese and the law article.

🏪 Simples Nacional calculator

The same idea for those under Simples. You enter the effective rate from the Simples calculation, and it calculates the CBS and IBS for each invoice item.

🖨️ DANFSe generator

É a printer for the service invoice. It receives the invoice file (XML) and returns the finished PDF, with a QR Code and receipt stub.

🔍 The official engine test

It’s the centerpiece of the kit. It works like checking a restaurant bill with your own calculator: you enter the same transaction in your system and in the Receita engine, and a table shows each item as "same" or "different," with the reason. You can also run the same sale with different rates and compare.

⚠️ What it is NOT

  • It isn’t law. The Ordinance itself says the code has no regulatory effect. The company remains responsible for the tax.
  • It isn’t supported by the Receita no promise of maintenance for the public, either.
  • It doesn’t update itself in the company's system: someone needs to bring in each new version and test it.
  • It doesn’t issue invoices. Calculates, checks the tax section, and generates the service invoice PDF.
  • The Receita name doesn’t come with it: the license lets you use the code, but not the name or symbols to claim a product is official.

  3. 16/11 · Receita announces the code on GitHub
    Previously, you could download it from the CBS Platform, the tax agency’s website for the new tax. Now there’s a history, versions, and the public can report errors.
  4. 17/11 · Fazenda cites an average effective rate of 8.5%
    The Minister of Finance said this at a Senate hearing. It’s an average estimate, different from the benchmark rate. It doesn’t mean every price changes by 8.5%.
  5. 19 and 20/11 · Both calculators have already received a new version
    The regular-regime calculator fixed the rounding: before, the total might not match the sum of the items, and the invoice would be rejected. A difference of a few cents for those already integrated.

Common questions

Before you get started

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You paste in the request, and the AI does the rest. For your software vendor or IT team, the README has the commands.

Is this a Receita website for looking things up?

No. The calculator is a program that runs on your computer: the calculation isn’t sent to the tax agency or to us. Note: if you use AI (Claude Code or Codex), anything it reads, including the spreadsheet with your sales, goes through its service. When using customer data, use sample sales or remove names and CNPJ numbers first, or ask your IT team to run it without AI.

If the engine gives a value, can I use it on the invoice?

It’s a strong reference, but it isn’t law, and in 2027 the result is labeled “simulated” until the official rates are entered. Use it to check your calculations, and talk to your accountant.

How much does it cost?

The kit, this guide, and the tax agency’s code (MIT free license) are free. The easiest way uses Claude Code or Codex, which come with a paid Claude or ChatGPT subscription. Without AI, your IT team can run everything at no software cost.

Does it work for businesses under Simples?

Yes, there's a calculator just for Simples. It needs the effective tax rate, which comes from the monthly tax calculation (PGDAS-D).

Can I sell a product made with this?

The MIT license allows this. You just can’t use Receita’s name or symbols to suggest the product is official, and you’re responsible for the calculation.
